About

I care about intent first, metrics second.

I'm an SEO Analyst and Web Content Specialist with experience in keyword research, keyword mapping, content strategy, and SEO analysis. I use keyword research to uncover the language, questions, and topics that matter most to an audience, then map those keywords to the right pages and content formats so the site is easy to discover and easy to navigate.

My approach combines search data, user intent, competitive research, and content relevance. By studying search results, competitor coverage, and content gaps, I find focused content opportunities that improve visibility and support sustainable organic growth. I carry that through to the page itself: titles, meta descriptions, headings, and the final draft.

AI-assisted, evidence-led

I use AI for research, ideation, and efficiency, then validate every insight with Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Semrush, SERP analysis, and real search data. That keeps the work practical and aligned with both audience needs and business goals.

A healthcare background most SEOs don't have

I studied nursing, trained as a medical coder (AAPC), and worked as a patient care coordinator and medical biller before moving into marketing. That makes it easier for me to write and optimize clinically sensitive content accurately and with the right tone.

YMCA of Delaware · Nonprofit / Youth programs

Optimizing an existing Swim Team page for local search

Keyword researchKeyword mappingOn-page SEOContent optimization
+9.4%organic traffic
188organic keywords

The /swim-team/ page already ranked, so the goal was to strengthen it rather than build something new. I reviewed its performance in Google Analytics and Semrush, found gaps in keyword targeting, and optimized the page without rewriting it from scratch.

  1. AuditReviewed traffic, ranking keywords, and visibility for the existing page.
  2. ResearchEvaluated new keywords on relevance, intent, competition, and audience fit.
  3. MapAssigned one primary target and supporting terms to the page.
  4. Optimize & measureUpdated metadata, headings, and copy, then rechecked in Semrush 2 to 3 months later.

Psychedelic Medicine Centers of America · Psychiatric & mental health care

From keyword cluster to a published blog on high-functioning depression

Keyword researchContent strategyKeyword mappingOn-page SEOEditing
22,200monthly searches, head term

PMC serves high-achieving professionals. My research surfaced strong demand around "high functioning depression," a topic tied to people who keep performing at work while struggling privately. I identified the shared search need behind the cluster and turned it into a content recommendation: educational, clinically grounded content that helps professionals recognize subtle symptoms and know when to seek evaluation.

  1. Find the opportunityAssessed volume, difficulty, intent, and fit with PMC's services.
  2. Set the angleDefined audience, intent, and content direction from the research.
  3. Map keywordsBuilt a focused Primary / Secondary / Supporting set with short rationales.
  4. Develop & publishWrote the SEO title, meta, H1, and structure; edited, approved, and published.

Primary

  • high functioning depression symptoms

Secondary

  • signs of high functioning depression

Supporting

  • high achievers and depression
  • executive high functioning depression
  • what does high functioning depression look like

On-page elements

High-Functioning Depression Symptoms in High Achievers: Subtle Signs to Recognize Learn the subtle signs of high-functioning depression in high-achieving professionals, including fatigue, detachment, and burnout, and when to seek evaluation.

Published H1: High Functioning Depression Symptoms in Professionals: What to Watch For

The article follows the research directly, with sections on what it looks like, why signs get missed, the cost of delayed care, psychiatric evaluation, discreet care, and treatment options.
